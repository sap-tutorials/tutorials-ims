## Who we are

This site (`developers.sap.com` Tutorial Platform) is operated by
SAP SE and its affiliates. The data controller for personal data
processed via the site depends on your country of residence — see the
"Who is the controller of your personal data?" section of the
[SAP Privacy Statement](https://www.sap.com/about/legal/privacy.html)
for the country-specific controller and contact address.

You can reach the SAP Group's Data Protection Officer at any time at
[privacy@sap.com](mailto:privacy@sap.com).

## What we collect and why

### Account data

When you sign in with your SAP account, we receive identifiers from the
SAP Identity Provider:

- A user identifier (subject ID)
- Your given name, family name, and email address

We use this data to:

- Display your name and avatar in the site header
- Personalise the Joule chat experience
- Associate your tutorial-progress records with your account

### Tutorial progress

When you mark a tutorial step complete, we store:

- The tutorial slug and step number
- A timestamp
- Your account identifier

This data enables progress tracking, completion paths, and event
leaderboards. It is retained for the lifetime of your account.

### Audit logs

We maintain audit logs for security and compliance purposes
(via the `@cap-js/audit-logging` framework). These logs record access to
personal data and are retained according to SAP retention policies.

### Joule chat

When you use the Joule assistant on the site, the messages you send are
processed by an AI model to generate responses. Chat transcripts are
stored in your browser's sessionStorage and are not sent to our servers
unless required to fulfil your request.

### What we do not collect

- We do not currently use analytics or advertising trackers on the site.
- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Legal basis for processing (EU/UK)

- **Contract** — to provide you with access to tutorials and progress
  tracking after you log in.
- **Legitimate interest** — to maintain audit logs and protect the site
  against abuse.
- **Consent** — for any future analytics or non-essential cookies, if and
  when introduced.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- Object to processing or request portability
- Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, use the
[SAP Privacy Statement contact form](https://www.sap.com/registration/contact.html?countryOfOrigin=en_us&navTitle=Contact%20Form&pageTitle=SAP%20Privacy%20Statement&refererContentPath=%2Fcontent%2Fsapdx%2Fcountries%2Fen_us%2Fabout%2Flegal%2Fprivacy&refererPagePath=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sap.com%2Fabout%2Flegal%2Fprivacy.html).
We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

## Account deletion

You can request deletion of your tutorial-progress data and account
records. The platform supports anonymisation of user records on request;
audit-log entries that reference your account will be retained as
required by law but will be detached from your identity where possible.

## International transfers

SAP SE is a global company, and your data may be processed in
jurisdictions other than your own. SAP applies the safeguards described
in the
[SAP Trust Center](https://www.sap.com/about/trust-center.html), including
Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.
